To reset your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot password." Enter the email address associated with your account. You'll receive a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder before requesting a new one.

If you're locked out after too many failed login attempts, your account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes as a security measure. Wait for the lock to expire, or contact support to have it lifted manually if you need immediate access.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled from Account Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS codes. Authenticator apps are recommended since SMS can be intercepted in rare cases. If you lose access to your 2FA device, use one of the backup codes generated when you first set up 2FA.

To change the email address on your account, go to Account Settings > Profile, enter the new email, and confirm it via the verification link sent to the new address. Your old email remains active until the new one is verified, so you won't be locked out mid-change.

Session tokens expire after 24 hours of inactivity. If you're being logged out unexpectedly sooner than that, check whether "Remember this device" was left unchecked at login, or whether multiple browser tabs are clearing cookies via a privacy extension.
