Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day each billing cycle, using the payment method on file. You'll get an email receipt within a few minutes of each successful charge.

To cancel a subscription, go to Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not refund the current billing period — you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds are issued for accidental duplicate charges or billing errors on our side, processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method. Refunds are not issued for partial months of an already-cancelled plan.

To update your card on file, go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Method. If a renewal fails because a card expired, we retry the charge three times over 7 days before downgrading the account to the free tier.

Enterprise invoicing (pay by invoice instead of card) is available for annual plans above a minimum seat count. Contact sales to switch a self-serve monthly plan to an annual invoiced contract.
